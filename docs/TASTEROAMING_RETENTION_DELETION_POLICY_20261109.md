## 1. Controller, scope and approved login model

- App: **TasteRoaming**, package `com.wonwinnie.taste_roaming`; developer/website identity: **WONWINNIE (원위니)**. Publishing business entity and responsible-person/contact identity require confirmation against Google Play/actual legal registration before final publication. Existing proposed contact: `support@wonwinnie.com` (mailbox delivery and responsible operator still unverified).
- Normal HERE, TRIP, WORLD, Battle, Recent, settings and map handoff do **not** require any application account or Google login.

The verified purchase belongs to the purchasing owner, not a phone. Reinstall/new Android device restoration requires matching purchasing accounts and **online** Google Play/backend verification. Refund/void/revocation may terminate entitlement. No payment-card details are collected by TasteRoaming's backend.
- No Google Analytics/Crashlytics service found in the reviewed Flutter dependency list. Do **not** claim no third-party collection: GMA/UMP, operating systems and external maps may separately process identifiers, location derived from IP, app activity or diagnostics.

## 3. Rights request and deletion workflow

1. User may contact `support@wonwinnie.com` to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of **WONWINNIE-controlled** data. No general app account is required. Provide clear scope (local-only vs purchase ledger vs email vs providers); never ask users to email purchase tokens, Google passwords, payment-card data or sensitive location trails.
2. Acknowledge under an **internal target of 3 business days** and promptly complete verified, legally permissible deletion, or explain any legal basis and necessary retention. This target is operational, **not a claim that law permits delay**. Authenticate purchase-ownership requests by safe Google Play/account-controlled evidence, not a user-supplied email address alone.
3. **Local-only:** Explain Android Settings → Apps → TasteRoaming → Storage → Clear storage/app data and relevant location/ad choices. This clears local app-controlled data on the current device but does not necessarily delete Android cloud backups or data held independently by Google/maps. Do not claim a non-existent in-app Clear Recent control.
4. **Purchase ledger:** Separate voluntary deletion of unnecessary data from cancellation/refund of a valid non-consumable purchase. A deletion request should not silently destroy an already purchased entitlement or force repurchase. Disclose what minimum records remain to preserve right to Restore, and offer an explicit, separately confirmed path if the user elects to terminate entitlement/purchase processing. Respect legal retention and avoid concealing its scope.
5. **Record deletion:** Restrict and segregate lawfully retained transaction/dispute evidence, record category, basis and disposal date; erase unnecessary HMAC/device bindings/token ciphertext and transient data when no longer needed and safe anti-replay/revocation logic is in place. Do not delete active unique token bindings ad hoc to reset idempotency.
6. Deletion must reach active Firestore collections and any copies/backups under the controller's control subject to realistic restore/expiry procedures. Document Cloud Firestore backup/PITR/export retention, role-based access, scheduled cleanup and a reproducible deletion test before claiming deletion complete. External Google Play data is managed through Google's own tools.
7. Retained data must be disposed of using a method that prevents feasible reconstruction. Privacy inquiries are not an authorization to refund a purchase, modify the storefront, or disable fraud controls without verification.

## 3A. Code-side retention progress (2026-11-09; not operating)

Unmerged purchase-verifier **Draft PR #22**, commit `a2d29d7feaa65ca149ce7989a64511e596479282`, now implements two deliberately **unexposed internal primitives**: (1) an explicit legal-hold-reviewed, time-cutoff-gated `minimize_revoked` transaction for confirmed revoked token rows only, which removes owner-HMAC and KMS-encrypted purchase token but retains a SHA-256 token-digest tombstone to block fraudulent replay; and (2) `unlink_verified_owner_device`, which removes a single previously authenticated device binding without deleting the valid purchase or requiring repurchase. Both require independent real operator/owner authorization; their boolean arguments **are not credentials or legal approval**. App/Cloud Run billing is disabled, and there is no public deletion endpoint, live cleanup scheduler or working mailbox deletion. Existing/legacy revoked rows without a revocation timestamp are skipped until a separately approved migration. The retained digest is potentially pseudonymous and is **not** a declaration of full erasure. Real retention decisions, lawful segregation, backups, device cleanup, live verification and final public policy approval remain blocked.

## 4. Android backup and app data policy

**Launch decision:** Prefer **no cloud or device-to-device restoration of TasteRoaming's user preferences and verifier proof**; users may reselect HERE/TRIP/maps and restore a valid Remove Ads purchase online using the original Google Play/billing identity. Food catalog/images are already bundled. Cloud backups must not function as a shadow retention channel for deleted Recent/recovery copies.

**Implementation status (2026-11-09):** Unmerged app Draft PR #22 now has explicit `android:allowBackup="false"`, Android 11- `fullBackupContent`, and Android 12+ `dataExtractionRules` with separate cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions for all supported private-storage domains (commit `275809c655f15a0446cd10f4a0c9e326fde5482a`). Android 12+ OEM behavior can vary if relying on allowBackup alone; XML exclusions are an additional explicit defense. **This code is not in the already installed internal Play versionCode=1, and actual merged manifests/device cloud-backup/D2D behavior still require CI and physical verification.** The policy is not effective merely because source code exists.
