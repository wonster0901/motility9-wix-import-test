## 4. TasteRoaming | Support /tasteroaming/support/

**Title:** TasteRoaming Support
**Lead:** Help with browsing dishes, map searches and app settings.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: TasteRoaming is designed for browsing its food catalog without a personal account. [Confirm against release build; do not publish if registration behavior changes.]

**Q: Can I browse food without internet access?**
A: The built-in catalog is available offline. External map services may require an internet connection.

**Q: Does TasteRoaming recommend verified restaurants?**
A: No. TasteRoaming opens a search for the selected dish in an external map service. Verify the restaurant location and whether the dish is offered directly with the venue.

**Q: Which map services can I use?**
A: Google Maps is the default. NAVER and Kakao can be used in supported Korean search contexts depending on the selected settings and device.

**Q: Why does a map search sometimes use an English dish name?**
A: TasteRoaming uses available verified local food names when the relevant search location is confirmed. If the location cannot be verified or a local search term is not available, an English dish name may be used.

**Q: How do I report a problem?**
A: Send the app version, your device model and the steps to reproduce the issue to support@wonwinnie.com. Do not email location history or other sensitive information unless needed for support.

**Contact:** support@wonwinnie.com
