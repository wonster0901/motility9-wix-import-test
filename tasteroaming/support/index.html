TasteRoaming Support

Help with browsing dishes, map searches and app settings.

Do I need to create an account?

TasteRoaming is designed for browsing its food catalog without a personal account.

Can I browse food without internet access?

The built-in catalog is available offline. External map services may require an internet connection.

Does TasteRoaming recommend verified restaurants?

No. TasteRoaming opens a search for the selected dish in an external map service. Verify the restaurant location and whether the dish is offered directly with the venue.

Which map services can I use?

Google Maps is the default. NAVER and Kakao can be used in supported Korean search contexts depending on the selected settings and device.

Why does a map search sometimes use an English dish name?

TasteRoaming uses available verified local food names when the relevant search location is confirmed. If the location cannot be verified or a local search term is not available, an English dish name may be used.

How do I report a problem?

Send the app version, your device model and the steps to reproduce the issue to support@wonwinnie.com. Do not email location history or other sensitive information unless needed for support.

How do I request deletion of app data?

Review TasteRoaming data-deletion request guidance (draft, not effective). The operator must confirm the mailbox and verified purchaser request process before enabling deletion.
