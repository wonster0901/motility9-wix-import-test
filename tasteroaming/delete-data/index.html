TasteRoaming Data Deletion Requests

What information TasteRoaming keeps and how a user may request access or deletion.

Developer: WONWINNIE (원위니) · App: TasteRoaming
Prepared: 9 November 2026 · Effective: 10 November 2026
Contact: support@wonwinnie.com

Do I need to delete an app account?

Ordinary HERE, TRIP, WORLD and Food Battle use does not require a TasteRoaming account or Google login. If Remove Ads is offered, Google identity may be requested to verify a purchase or Restore. You may ask us to review or delete information held by WONWINNIE for purchase verification through the contact route below. Deleting a Google account itself is handled by Google.

Delete information stored on your phone

Recent food IDs, selected region, maps preference and similar settings are primarily on your device. You can delete TasteRoaming app data using Android Settings → Apps → TasteRoaming → Storage → Clear storage (wording can vary), or uninstall the app. An older Android cloud backup may still exist: uninstalling does not necessarily delete every backup. A future app update includes more restrictive Android backup and device-transfer settings, but those changes are not yet in the installed Play internal test version.

Request deletion of information held by WONWINNIE

Email support@wonwinnie.com with the subject “TasteRoaming privacy or data deletion request”. Identify whether you want information regarding support correspondence, purchase ownership or a previously authorized device. We aim to acknowledge within three business days and explain any required verification and applicable lawful retention. Do not email passwords, raw purchase tokens, payment-card data or identity tokens. The sender's email address alone is insufficient to prove ownership of a Play purchase. This is a manual request route, not a claim of instantaneous server deletion.

What happens to a paid Remove Ads purchase?

A valid one-time Remove Ads purchase is intended to remain restorable on a replacement Android device without a second payment. Removing an old device authorization is different from cancelling/refunding that purchase. Deleting a minimal purchase-owner record while the entitlement is still valid could prevent Restore; a verified user must be informed of the implications and any lawful necessity to retain data. Refund or entitlement termination is a separate action, not an automatic side effect of a privacy inquiry.

Retention, exceptions and third parties

Our support retention policy reviews ordinary resolved correspondence for removal from the active mailbox 90 days after resolution, subject to legal holds or applicable transaction/dispute records. Zoho Mail Lite may retain deleted mail in Trash or recovery storage for longer. Some consumer dispute records may have a three-year retention period, and applicable transaction/payment records a five-year period under Korean law. Google Play, AdMob, maps, Android and other providers may separately hold data under their own rules.

When purchase verification is active, Firestore scheduled backups may preserve data in snapshots for up to seven days even after it has been deleted from the active database; point-in-time recovery is disabled. A verified valid Remove Ads purchase is handled separately from removing unnecessary data, and refund or entitlement termination is not an automatic effect of a privacy inquiry.

Other privacy information
