TasteRoaming Data Deletion Requests

What information TasteRoaming keeps and how a user may request access or deletion once the process is approved.

Developer: WONWINNIE (원위니) · App: TasteRoaming
Prepared: 9 November 2026 · Effective date: not set
Proposed contact: support@wonwinnie.com

Do I need to delete an app account?

Ordinary HERE, TRIP, WORLD, map search and food browsing do not require a TasteRoaming account or login. If the optional Remove Ads feature becomes available, the app will request Google identity only for a purchase or explicit Restore. Whether this limited ownership binding meets Google Play's definition of app account creation requires final store-policy review. This page is a preparation measure, not a claim that account deletion has already been implemented.

Delete information stored on your phone

Recent food IDs, selected region, maps preference and similar settings are primarily on your device. You can delete TasteRoaming app data using Android Settings → Apps → TasteRoaming → Storage → Clear storage (wording can vary), or uninstall the app. An older Android cloud backup may still exist: uninstalling does not necessarily delete every backup. A future app update includes more restrictive Android backup and device-transfer settings, but those changes are not yet in the installed Play internal test version.

Request deletion of information held by WONWINNIE

Once the support process has been verified, requests for access, correction or deletion can be initiated at support@wonwinnie.com. Specify “TasteRoaming privacy/deletion request” and whether it concerns a support message, purchase owner record, or device authorization. Do not email a Google password, raw Play purchase token, payment-card details, identity token, or sensitive location history. We must verify the actual purchasing owner before making changes to billing or device authorization data; an email address alone is not proof of purchase ownership. The proposed acknowledgement target is three business days, subject to final mailbox and operator setup.

What happens to a paid Remove Ads purchase?

A valid one-time Remove Ads purchase is intended to remain restorable on a replacement Android device without a second payment. Removing an old device authorization is different from cancelling/refunding that purchase. Deleting a minimal purchase-owner record while the entitlement is still valid could prevent Restore; a verified user must be informed of the implications and any lawful necessity to retain data. Refund or entitlement termination is a separate action, not an automatic side effect of a privacy inquiry.

Retention, exceptions and third parties

The proposed operating policy reviews routine support correspondence for active-mailbox removal 90 days after the inquiry is resolved, unless there is a lawful hold. The current support provider is Zoho Mail Lite, whose Trash/server recovery periods may extend beyond that business disposal date. Where Korean e-commerce law applies, certain transaction records may be retained for five years and formal consumer complaint/dispute records for three years. Data held for a specific legal or fraud-prevention purpose will be separately limited and explained. Google Play, AdMob, external maps and Android providers may maintain independent records under their own controls. Our current purchase-verification backend has no operating customer deletion endpoint and no proven automated purge or backup propagation. This page will be updated before any deletion process is offered as operational.

Other privacy information

Review the TasteRoaming privacy notice draft and TasteRoaming support information. Both the privacy policy and this proposed request route require final approval before public use.
