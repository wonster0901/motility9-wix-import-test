From food discovery to map search.

See the name, image and story of a dish, then open a search in an external map service.

Google Maps is available for map handoff. NAVER and Kakao are options in supported Korean search contexts.

A food collection you can browse offline.

The built-in dish catalog is available without a network connection. Opening a search in an external map service may require connectivity.

TasteRoaming does not maintain a verified list of restaurants or guarantee that a place serves a selected dish. Search results, locations and menu availability depend on the map provider.
