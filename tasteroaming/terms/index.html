TasteRoaming Terms of Use — release candidate

Terms for food browsing, map searches, ads and optional one-time purchases.

Developer: WONWINNIE (원위니) · App: TasteRoaming
Prepared: 7 November 2026 · Effective date: not set
Contact: support@wonwinnie.com

1. About TasteRoaming

TasteRoaming is provided by WONWINNIE (원위니) for discovering food through HERE, TRIP and WORLD. The bundled catalog can be browsed offline; external map searches may require an internet connection.

2. Food content and external maps

Dish descriptions and images are for discovery only, not guarantees of ingredients, allergens, nutrition, origin or restaurant availability. TasteRoaming does not operate a verified restaurant directory, provide table reservations or confirm that an external venue serves a dish. Verify menus, ingredients, suitability and directions directly with the venue. External map providers set their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Advertising and optional purchase

Core food browsing remains free without a required personal account. When enabled, the app may show ads. An optional one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase is planned; it is neither a subscription nor a food-content unlock. Any offered purchase shows the current Google Play price and requires verification of ownership for initial access and restoration. The prerelease billing implementation proposes a separate Google identity authorization only within explicit purchase/restore flows, not during normal browsing. This billing-only authentication design is pending final approval and is not currently active.

Google Play payment procedures and applicable consumer law govern cancellation and refunds. These terms do not waive statutory rights. A Cloud Run purchase-verification preview was deployed on 8 November 2026 with billing deliberately disabled. This deployment does not enable real purchases, restores or refunds; the prerelease app still does not offer live purchases. Final operational recovery and offline-entitlement rules will be disclosed before activation.

4. Appropriate use

Use the app and linked services lawfully. Respect intellectual property and the applicable rules of external providers. Do not misuse or interfere with the app, or describe map results as independently verified by WONWINNIE.

5. Data and device responsibility

Your Recent entries and preferences are generally local to your device and may be lost if app data is cleared or the device changes. Cloud recovery of local food history is not promised. Device backups may behave differently. See the TasteRoaming Privacy Policy for the app's data handling and provider disclosures.

6. Availability and updates

The service, catalog and external integrations may change. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted third-party maps, internet connections, advertising or billing services. Changes to these terms will be communicated where required; the final effective date will be set when they are published.

7. Contact

For questions, contact WONWINNIE (원위니) at support@wonwinnie.com.
