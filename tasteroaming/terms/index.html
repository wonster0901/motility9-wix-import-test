TasteRoaming Terms — review draft

Proposed service terms for owner and legal review. These are not final terms for a released app.

Developer: WONWINNIE · App: TasteRoaming
Review date: 7 November 2026 · Effective date: not set
Privacy inquiries: support@wonwinnie.com

1. Service description

TasteRoaming is developed by WONWINNIE to help people discover foods using HERE, TRIP and WORLD. Its bundled catalog supports offline browsing. External map handoffs may require connectivity and third-party services.

2. Food information and external providers

Food names, images and descriptions support discovery. TasteRoaming does not maintain verified restaurant listings, provide booking services or guarantee that a venue serves a selected dish. Check locations, menus, ingredients and dietary suitability directly with the venue. External services have their own terms and privacy practices.

3. Core features, advertising and purchases

All food and core exploration features remain free under the current product policy. An optional one-time non-consumable Remove Ads purchase is planned, with restoration of verified purchases. It is not a subscription or a consumable-credit product.

Production ads, the Play product and trusted verification service are not connected. Final purchase availability, store-provided prices, restoration, entitlement verification and offline behavior must be confirmed before these terms are effective. This website does not sell or activate a product.

4. Purchases and refunds

Where a Google Play purchase is actually offered, the app should display the store-provided price and purchase information. Google Play procedures and applicable law govern relevant payment and refund rights. This draft does not waive statutory rights or impose a blanket no-refund rule.

5. Appropriate use and content

Use the app and external services lawfully and respect applicable content rights. Food discovery content and the software are subject to the rights of their respective owners. Specific licensing and acceptable-use wording requires final review.

6. Availability, changes and local data

Features and catalog information may be updated. Local Recent and settings depend on device storage; this draft does not promise cloud synchronization or recovery. Review the app-specific privacy draft for confirmed behavior and unresolved operational details.

Final service availability, limitations, applicable jurisdiction and legal terms remain subject to owner/legal approval. No launch date is promised.

7. Contact

For TasteRoaming questions, contact support@wonwinnie.com.
