TasteRoaming Privacy Policy

How TasteRoaming handles local preferences, optional location, external maps, advertising, purchases and privacy inquiries.

Developer: WONWINNIE (원위니) · App: TasteRoaming
Prepared: 7 November 2026 · Effective: 10 November 2026
Contact: support@wonwinnie.com

1. Operator and service

TasteRoaming is a food-discovery Android app provided by WONWINNIE (원위니), Republic of Korea. It allows users to browse bundled food entries and images through HERE, TRIP and WORLD and to search for selected foods through external map services. A personal account, name, email address or telephone number is not required for basic browsing. This notice applies to TasteRoaming, not MOTILITY9.

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores up to twenty visible recently selected dish IDs, manually chosen HERE country, automatic-country preference, TRIP destination, preferred map app and other app settings locally on your device. It can also store ad display/frequency records and, if a verified purchase feature is activated, a limited entitlement proof. Some old values may remain in bounded local recovery copies following a migration. The app does not currently upload browsing history to a WONWINNIE account, and does not promise additional encryption for all local preferences.

3. Optional location

Automatic country identification is optional. You may choose a country manually without location access. After you opt in, the app can ask Android for approximate foreground location and use device geocoding to identify your country. Nearby map search may check your physical country with already-granted permission. The app does not request background location permission or intentionally maintain a long-term raw-coordinate history. Temporary location fixes expire; Android and location/geocoding providers may process location data under their own privacy terms. You may withdraw location permission in Android Settings without losing access to the bundled catalog.

4. External map services

When you tap Find, the app opens a food search using Google Maps or, in supported Korean search contexts, NAVER or Kakao. The query may include the dish name, the selected destination or, for nearby searches, fresh location context. TRIP uses the chosen destination instead of device GPS position. An external map app, website and browser may separately collect or process query, location and device data under their own policies. TasteRoaming does not verify venue menus or guarantee that a restaurant serves a selected dish.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The free app may display advertising. Where enabled, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and security. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. Collection and sharing depend on the device, jurisdiction, settings and privacy choices. When required, the app presents a consent message and a Privacy Options control. Refusing optional personalized advertising does not block the core food catalog, although required or non-personalized data processing by third-party services may still occur.

6. Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Normal food browsing does not require an account or Google sign-in. When an optional non-consumable Remove Ads purchase or Restore is offered, Google Play handles payment; WONWINNIE does not request payment-card details. Google identity is used only for an explicit purchase or Restore, to verify the purchasing owner and valid entitlement. A valid purchase is intended to remain restorable on a replacement Android device without a second payment, subject to online verification; refunds or revocations may terminate the entitlement.

Purchase verification may temporarily handle a Google ID token and use a pseudonymous owner identifier, Google Play purchase token hash or encrypted token, SKU, purchase/acknowledgement/refund status, device public-key digest and Play Integrity evidence. The verified purchase ledger is designed not to retain Google email/profile as the purchaser-ownership claim. Minimum owner and entitlement records may be retained while a purchase remains valid, and necessary transaction/dispute evidence may be kept separately where required by law. The purchase service and offline verification features are available only when supported by the installed app and operating backend.

7. Support information

When you contact support@wonwinnie.com, we receive your sender address, message and any details you voluntarily provide. Our policy is to review routine correspondence for deletion from the active mailbox 90 days after resolution unless a legal hold or applicable retention obligation applies. Zoho Mail Lite processes support mail; deleted emails may remain in its Trash or recovery storage for a further provider-controlled period. Consumer disputes or transaction records may be retained for three or five years respectively where Korean e-commerce law applies. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, raw purchase tokens or sensitive location information.

8. Providers, sharing and international processing

Depending on the features you use, relevant providers include Android location/geocoding services, Google Maps, NAVER and Kakao, Google Mobile Ads and UMP, Google Play purchases, Google Cloud for purchase verification when enabled, and Zoho Mail for customer support. These providers may process information outside the Republic of Korea under their own privacy policies and applicable law. WONWINNIE does not sell your on-device food history to restaurants. This does not mean that independent maps, ads or OS providers collect no identifiers or data. Contact the privacy address below for questions about data processing or international transfers.

9. Storage, deletion and rights

Recent food selections, manually chosen HERE and TRIP destinations, map preference, advertising frequency records and limited migration recovery copies are stored locally until overwritten, cleared via Android app data controls or removed through the device's lifecycle. To clear the current phone's data, open Android Settings → Apps → TasteRoaming → Storage → Clear storage/data; you may also uninstall. The app has no dedicated Clear Recent button. Recent Android build sources include cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions, but earlier Android/cloud backups may still exist; uninstalling an app does not necessarily erase copies held by a device or service provider. Location permission can be withdrawn in Android Settings.

For access, correction or deletion of information controlled by WONWINNIE, write to support@wonwinnie.com or see our data-deletion request instructions. State whether your request concerns an email inquiry, purchase-owner record or device authorization; do not provide passwords or purchase tokens. Purchase-owner requests require secure identity verification beyond the sender email address. Deleting a device binding is different from cancelling a valid Remove Ads purchase, and the minimum records needed to preserve your right to Restore may still be needed. We aim to acknowledge a request within three business days and explain lawful retention exceptions.

When purchase verification data is held in Firestore, it may also be present in scheduled daily backups retained for up to seven days; point-in-time recovery is disabled. Deleting live data may not immediately erase copies in existing snapshots, and applicable transaction/dispute records can be retained separately. Data held independently by Google, external maps or other providers remains subject to their controls.

10. Security and updates

The app uses Android app storage and permission controls. Providers apply their respective network security measures; Google says its advertising SDK transmits collected data using encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when the app's functions or data processing practices change.

11. Privacy contact

Publisher and privacy contact: WONWINNIE (원위니), Republic of Korea. Email: support@wonwinnie.com. Relevant independent provider information includes the Google Privacy Policy and UMP privacy guidance.
