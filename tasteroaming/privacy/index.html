TasteRoaming Privacy Policy — release candidate

How TasteRoaming handles local preferences, optional location, external maps, advertising, purchases and privacy inquiries.

Developer: WONWINNIE (원위니) · App: TasteRoaming
Prepared: 7 November 2026 · Effective date: not set
Contact: support@wonwinnie.com

1. Operator and service

TasteRoaming is a food-discovery Android app provided by WONWINNIE (원위니), Republic of Korea. It allows users to browse bundled food entries and images through HERE, TRIP and WORLD and to search for selected foods through external map services. A personal account, name, email address or telephone number is not required for basic browsing. This notice applies to TasteRoaming, not MOTILITY9.

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores up to twenty visible recently selected dish IDs, manually chosen HERE country, automatic-country preference, TRIP destination, preferred map app and other app settings locally on your device. It can also store ad display/frequency records and, if a verified purchase feature is activated, a limited entitlement proof. Some old values may remain in bounded local recovery copies following a migration. The app does not currently upload browsing history to a WONWINNIE account, and does not promise additional encryption for all local preferences.

3. Optional location

Automatic country identification is optional. You may choose a country manually without location access. After you opt in, the app can ask Android for approximate foreground location and use device geocoding to identify your country. Nearby map search may check your physical country with already-granted permission. The app does not request background location permission or intentionally maintain a long-term raw-coordinate history. Temporary location fixes expire; Android and location/geocoding providers may process location data under their own privacy terms. You may withdraw location permission in Android Settings without losing access to the bundled catalog.

4. External map services

When you tap Find, the app opens a food search using Google Maps or, in supported Korean search contexts, NAVER or Kakao. The query may include the dish name, the selected destination or, for nearby searches, fresh location context. TRIP uses the chosen destination instead of device GPS position. An external map app, website and browser may separately collect or process query, location and device data under their own policies. TasteRoaming does not verify venue menus or guarantee that a restaurant serves a selected dish.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

When live advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) support ad delivery and relevant privacy choices. Under Google's published SDK disclosures, Google may collect or share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, app/ad interaction data and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and security. The exact processing can vary by location, device, consent choices and SDK configuration. Where required, the app offers a privacy message and an available Privacy Options control; refusal of an advertising purpose does not block the core food catalog. The reviewed prerelease app used test-only advertising and had not connected live ad serving.

6. Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Core food browsing does not require a personal account or Google sign-in. On 9 November 2026, WONWINNIE approved Google identity authorization only when you explicitly purchase or restore the optional non-consumable Remove Ads item. Google Play handles payment; WONWINNIE does not request payment-card details. A still-valid purchase is intended to be restorable on the same or a replacement Android device without a second payment, after online verification of the original Play purchasing account and authenticated owner. Purchases remain disabled in the prerelease app.

If billing is activated, the purchase verifier is designed to process a Google ID token transiently to derive a pseudonymous owner identifier; Google Play purchase token and status, its hash and encrypted token, SKU, entitlement/acknowledgement/refund state, registered device public-key digest and Play Integrity evidence. No Google email/profile is stored as the purchase-owner claim in the reviewed ledger. The minimum ownership and purchase-verification record is intended to be retained while a valid purchase must remain restorable; terminated purchases may require legally mandated transaction/dispute evidence, separated and retained only for the applicable period. The current server implementation has no full user-data purge/retention lifecycle and billing remains disabled; such handling must be implemented and confirmed before the policy becomes effective. A short-lived online proof is not an offline purchase guarantee.

7. Support information

If you email support@wonwinnie.com, we receive your sender address, message and any app/device details you voluntarily provide. The proposed operational rule is to keep routine resolved inquiries for up to 90 days after resolution, then remove them from our active mailbox unless a specific legal obligation or active dispute requires separate retention. Zoho Mail Lite is the current support-mail provider; deleted mail may remain in Zoho Trash and provider recovery storage for additional periods under Zoho's separate settings. Consumer complaint/dispute evidence may require three years of retention, and applicable sales/payment records may require five years under Korean e-commerce rules. We do not intend to store unrelated sensitive details. Actual Zoho Mail Lite mailbox settings, deletion review procedure and provider recovery lifecycle must be verified before the rule is effective; a 90-day business-retention target is not a guarantee of permanent provider erasure at day 90.

8. Providers, sharing and international processing

Depending on app use, parties involved in processing may include Android location/geocoding providers, Google Maps, NAVER, Kakao, Google Mobile Ads/UMP, Google Play Billing, a future verified purchase service and our email provider for voluntary support messages. Those services may process data outside your country, subject to their terms and applicable legal requirements. WONWINNIE does not directly provide local browsing records to restaurants, but this does not mean that advertising SDKs or map providers collect no data. Additional legally required international transfer notices will be provided before activation.

9. Storage, deletion and rights

Recent dish IDs, locally chosen countries and destinations, map preference, ad frequency records and limited legacy recovery copies remain on the device until overwritten, explicitly cleared or removed through Android app-data controls. TasteRoaming does not currently offer a dedicated in-app Clear Recent button or personal app-account deletion flow. Android backups and device-to-device transfers may retain some local data after uninstall: source-level backup-exclusion rules have now been added to an unpublished app development branch, but they are not yet deployed or verified on actual Android cloud-backup/device-transfer behavior. Removing the app alone therefore cannot be represented as deletion of all backups. You can disable location permission in Android Settings and manage advertising privacy choices where available.

For access, correction or deletion of information controlled by WONWINNIE, contact support@wonwinnie.com. A TasteRoaming data-deletion request route is being prepared but is not yet an effective operational service. We will distinguish local-only information, support email and, if enabled, verified purchase records. Purchase-data deletion must not silently cancel a paid, still-valid non-consumable right; minimum owner/entitlement data may need to be kept for Restore, and statutory records retained separately. We propose initial acknowledgement within three business days and prompt lawful action after reasonable identity verification; actual procedures remain subject to readiness review. Records no longer needed must be securely deleted, with legal retention grounds disclosed when relevant. Information independently held by Google Play, AdMob, Android/geocoding or external maps is subject to those providers' controls.

For the proposed purchase service, a daily Firestore backup schedule with seven-day retention has been configured, while point-in-time recovery remains disabled. At the last operator check on 10 November 2026, no backup snapshot was yet available and an isolated restoration had not been tested. If later backups contain personal records, deletion from the active database does not necessarily immediately remove retained snapshot copies; such copies are subject to their backup retention period and applicable legal requirements. No live purchases are enabled in the reviewed release.

10. Security and updates

The app uses Android app storage and permission controls. Providers apply their respective network security measures; Google says its advertising SDK transmits collected data using encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security. This notice will be updated when the release configuration or data handling changes. An effective date will be assigned upon publication.

11. Privacy contact

Publisher and privacy contact: WONWINNIE (원위니), Republic of Korea. Email: support@wonwinnie.com. Relevant independent provider information includes the Google Privacy Policy and UMP privacy guidance.
