TasteRoaming Privacy Policy — review draft

A review of the current prerelease app and the operational details that must be confirmed before a final notice takes effect.

Developer: WONWINNIE · App: TasteRoaming
Review date: 7 November 2026 · Effective date: not set
Privacy inquiries: support@wonwinnie.com

1. Scope and current status

TasteRoaming is an offline-first food discovery app developed by WONWINNIE. It is separate from MOTILITY9 and does not use MOTILITY9’s clinical-record or Report Credit Wallet service. The reviewed app does not require a personal account to browse its catalog.

TasteRoaming has not been publicly released. Current code includes location, external maps, Google advertising/consent SDKs and Play Billing components. Their presence does not establish that production advertising or purchasing is enabled.

2. Food browsing and information stored on the device

The food catalog and approved images are bundled with the app. The Recent view shows up to twenty unique dish IDs. Preferences include the manual HERE country, automatic-country preference, selected TRIP destination and preferred map service.

Local storage also supports advertising completion/limit records and, when supplied by a configured verifier, an opaque purchase-verification proof. Migration or recovery copies of earlier saved values may remain on the device. This draft does not claim that local preferences are separately encrypted by the app.

3. Foreground location and country confirmation

Current-country detection is optional. You can select a country manually without location permission. After an explicit automatic-location opt-in, the app can confirm the country in the foreground using an existing permission. A nearby map search can also reconfirm physical country with already-granted permission, independently of a manually selected recommendation country.

The reviewed Android manifest requests approximate foreground location. The app obtains a bounded location fix and uses the device geocoding service to identify a country. Location providers may process coordinates under their own policies. The app does not add background location tracking or persist raw coordinates as location history. Active coordinates are short-lived and are invalidated on background transitions or expiry.

4. Searches sent to external maps

When you choose to find a dish, the app passes a food query and relevant search context to an external map app or website. HERE, WORLD and Recent can use confirmed physical location and fresh coordinates. TRIP uses the selected destination rather than the device’s GPS position.

Google Maps is the default; NAVER and Kakao are available for supported Korean search contexts. Native links and web fallbacks are used. These providers and your browser may process queries, location, network or device information under their own policies. TasteRoaming does not verify restaurant menus or guarantee local search results.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The reviewed code contains Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Production ad serving is not configured. Isolated development builds can request official Google test ads only when their build settings allow it and UMP reports that ads may be requested.

Google’s SDKs may process identifiers, network/device information, interactions and diagnostics according to their current disclosures and configuration. UMP itself can contact Google to update consent information or load a form. A privacy-options control is provided in Settings when required by the SDK.

Before publication, verify the final SDK disclosures, production AdMob IDs, consent messages, applicable regions, personalization behavior, provider data use and Google Play Data safety declarations. This draft does not treat UMP permission to request ads as a universal statement that every user consented to personalized advertising.

6. Google Play purchases and restoration

Optional Remove Ads is designed as a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription or consumable credit balance. The production product ID and trusted verification service are not connected in the reviewed app, and production purchasing remains blocked.

The final server contract must identify how purchase tokens, package/product IDs, purchase states, ownership binding, acknowledgements, refunds and restoration records are processed. Server operator, authentication, retention, deletion and device-change recovery remain unconfirmed. No operational backend or permanent entitlement is implied by this draft.

7. Support and other diagnostics

If you email support, your address, message and any voluntarily supplied app/device information are used to address your inquiry. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. No separate WONWINNIE analytics or crash-reporting service was identified in the reviewed app; third-party SDKs and the operating system may still process their own diagnostics.

The support mailbox’s operational access, retention period and deletion process require owner confirmation. This review does not independently verify the email provider’s handling or delivery.

8. Retention, deletion and device backups

Recent and settings are local app data. The reviewed app does not provide an account-deletion screen or a dedicated Clear Recent control. Android’s app-data controls can remove local app storage; doing so removes saved choices and settings.

Confirm Android backup/restore behavior and guidance before finalizing deletion claims. The reviewed manifest does not explicitly disable platform backup. Do not assume uninstalling guarantees removal of platform backups. Retention and deletion rules for future purchase servers and support records are not yet approved.

9. Security and service providers

App-local data uses the platform’s app storage mechanisms. Maps handoffs and provider SDKs are governed by their respective services; no device or network can guarantee absolute security. The app must not contain Play service-account secrets, and this draft does not assert that unconfigured server-side security has been implemented.

Named service categories include device location/geocoding, Google Maps, NAVER Map, KakaoMap, Google Mobile Ads/UMP and Google Play Billing. Their exact operational roles, data categories and retention must be reconciled with the final configuration. This is not a blanket “no collection” or “no sharing” claim.

10. Review and contact

Before this draft becomes an effective policy, WONWINNIE must approve the operational details, review applicable privacy requirements, align Google Play disclosures and verify an accessible public HTTPS page linked from the app. No final privacy URL is submitted through this website implementation.

Privacy questions: support@wonwinnie.com
